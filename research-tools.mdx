## Relevant Plans / Reports

<Card title="Cheated by Car Dependence: Drivers and Nondrivers in the United States" icon="car" horizontal href="https://climateandcommunity.org/research/driver-or-nondriver/">
  This report from Climate and Community Institute, America Walks, and Nondrivers Alliance draws on national polling from Data for Progress, analysis of US Census microdata, and interviews with drivers and nondrivers. It shows who is paying the price of car dependence, and how policymakers can give everyone real choices in how they get around.
</Card>
