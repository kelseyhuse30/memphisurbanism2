<Card title="Better Transit for Better Memphis (BTBM)" icon="bus-alt" href="https://www.micahmemphis.org/transit-equity">
  This group is organized under MICAH to fight for more funding and better services for MATA riders in Memphis.

  They hold a monthly meeting every 3rd Thursday from 5:30-7pm in-person at Idlewild Presbyterian Church and on Zoom. Please contact them at [bettertransit@micahmemphis.org](mailto:bettertransit@micahmemphis.org) to RSVP.

  Each year they hold a Transit Equity Day event that occurs near Rosa Park's birthday, and is attended by MATA leadership, local elected officials, and community leaders.
</Card>

<Card title="Disability Connection Midsouth" icon="wheelchair" href="https://www.disabilitymidsouth.org/">
  This group can often be seen with BTBM, and advocates specifically for people with disabilities in the Memphis region.
</Card>
