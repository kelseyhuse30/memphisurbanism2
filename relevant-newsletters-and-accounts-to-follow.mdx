<Info>
  Want to be an informed Memphian? Here are some newsletters and accounts that I find helpful, and where I source information from.
</Info>

# Memphis Metro Newsletters

<Card title="Smart City Memphis" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.smartcitymemphis.com/">
  Tom Jones, and other guest writers, contribute to in-depth reporting on urbanism in Memphis. Highly recommend subscribing!
</Card>

<Card title="Mayor Young Weekly Updates" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://memphistn.gov/weekly-updates/">
  The mayor's weekly newsletter about what the city has been delivering for its residents, and what he's thinking about lately.
</Card>

<Card title="Memphis Flyer Weekly Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.memphisflyer.com/newsletters/">
  "Want the latest in local news, events, music and food? Our free newsletters deliver Memphis straight to your inbox."
</Card>

<Card title="Memphis MPO Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://memphismpo.org/news-events/newsletter">
  From their website> The **_Travel Times_** is the official newsletter of the Memphis Urban Area MPO. The MPO uses this newsletter to keep the public informed about transportation projects, transportation issues, and provides a calendar of upcoming transportation meetings.
</Card>

<Card title="MLK50 Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://mlk50.com/about/subscribe/">
  MLK50's slogan is "justice through journalism". They do the investigative work that other local news organizations are not doing; especially regarding housing and transportation.
</Card>

<Card title="The Institute for Public Service Reporting Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.psrmemphis.org/newsletter/">
  PSR Memphis' > "Our focus is deeply researched watchdog journalism and narrative, documentary journalism distributed through variety of local, regional and national media partners."
</Card>

<Card title="Memphis Public Library Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.memphislibrary.org/about/email-signup/">
  Sign up for the Memphis Public Library monthly newsletter. They have so many events, including repair clinics, technology training, and book clubs.
</Card>

<Card title="Downtown Memphis Commission Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://downtownmemphis.us2.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=1d03531616819d433554ef5b2&id=8880f8fb4b">
  Sign up for the weekly Downtown Memphis Commission (DMC) newsletter to hear about upcoming events happening downtown.
</Card>

## Community Group Newsletters

<Card title="MICAH Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.micahmemphis.org/newsletter">
  Memphis Interfaith Coalition for Action and Hope (MICAH) is "equipping people of faith and goodwill to organize communities for systemic change through collective action."
</Card>

<Card title="Protect our Aquifer Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.protectouraquifer.org/">
  Sign up form is at the bottom of their website.
</Card>

## Political Newsletters

I find political newsletters interesting, just to get a pulse on different opinions and ideas. I don't necessarily endorse their ideas or platforms, but I think they can be helpful to understand different political views in Memphis.

<Card title="The 3AM" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.the-3am.com/subscribe">
  Mike Carpenter calls his newsletter "The smartest briefing on national, Tennessee, and Memphis politics—written by someone who actually knows how power works."
</Card>

<Card title="Jackson Baker Politics" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://jacksonbakerpolitics.substack.com/">
  Jackson Baker's bio > "Longtime political editor and columnist, Memphis Flyer; stringer for TIME Magazine; contributing editor. Tennessee zJournsl; commentator, WREG"
</Card>

<Card title="John Branston Substack" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://johnbranston.substack.com/">
  John Branston's bio > "Former reporter and columnist in memphis and Mississippi. I worked for UPI in Nashville and Jackson, MS. Also for Mid-South magazine, Memphis magazine and wrote a column for The Flyer, books Rowdy Memphis and What Katy Did."
</Card>

<Card title="Rachel and the City" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://rachelandthecity.com/blog/">
  Rachel Hurley describes her website/blog with this bio > "Thirty years in media, and now I spend my days on people who are counting on nobody checking. Russian money and an American presidency. Epstein’s enablers. An AI company that built the world’s biggest computer in my city. Different stories, same habit. Everything here is built on documents you can open yourself."
</Card>

# National Newsletters

<Card title="Strong Towns Newsletter" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://www.strongtowns.org/email">
  Strong Towns is a National organization that advocates for cities of all sizes to be safe, livable and inviting. Their newsletter includes Insights, Reflections, and Hot Takes on the Stories Shaping Our Cities and Towns.
</Card>

<Card title="Beth Osborne Substack" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://bethosbornesga.substack.com/subscribe">
  Beth Osborne is one of my heroes and a transportation policy visionary. Her newsletter is really insightful regarding National transportation priorities.
</Card>

<Card title="Joan Lyons Substack" icon="newspaper" horizontal href="https://womenmovingpeople.substack.com/subscribe">
  Joan Lyons (also known as women moving people) writes poetry and observations about urbanism.
</Card>
