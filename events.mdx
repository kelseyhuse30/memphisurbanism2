<Expandable title="Thursday, Oct 1-8 - International Week Without Driving">
  Week without Driving is an international week where people commit to not driving themselves, relying instead on transit, walking, biking, or getting rides from people. People document their experiences to build empathy and awareness of the experience.<br /><br />Learn more [here](https://weekwithoutdriving.org/).
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Saturday, Oct 17 - The Legacy of 87 Adams">
  87 Adams is a memorial and public history project honoring the lives of the estimated 3,800 enslaved people who were bought and sold in the shadows of historic churches in Downtown Memphis. They are holding 3 historical tours in downtown Memphis with artists.<br /><br />RSVP [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-legacy-of-87-adams-program-and-historical-tour-tickets-2000203126395?aff=oddtdtcreator).
