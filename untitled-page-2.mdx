<Info>
  None of these bike shops have paid me to be highlighted here, or have influenced any of the wording on this page.
</Info>

<Card title="Dos Gatos - 1535 Overton Park Ave, Memphis, TN 38112" icon="bicycle" href="https://dosgatosbikeshop.com/">
  This new bike shop has excellent maintenance and customer service right in the heart of midtown. You'll find tuned up and rebuilt used bikes for sale here, and a TON of accessory options.
</Card>

<Card title="All About Bikes - 4904 Poplar Ave, Memphis, TN 38117" icon="bicycle" href="https://www.allaboutbikesllc.com/">
  This East Memphis bike shop has a variety of affordable bike options, including e-bikes and bikes for kids. They have a great maintenance staff and accessories too.
</Card>

<Card title="Victory Bicycle Studio - 2549 Broad Ave, Memphis, TN 38112" icon="bicycle" href="https://victorybicyclestudio.com">
  Located on Broad Ave, Victory caters toward the high-end bike crowd. If you want a super quality bike, this is your place. They also have great maintenance staff and a lot of accessories.
</Card>

<Card title="Frayser Bicycle - 3740 N Watkins St Ste C, Memphis, TN 38127" icon="bicycle" href="https://www.frayserbicycle.com/">
  I haven't yet visited this new shop in Frayser, but they deserve our support. Once I go there I'll update this card with more info about them.
</Card>

<Card title="REI - 5897 Poplar Avenue, Memphis, TN 38119" icon="bicycle" href="https://www.rei.com/stores/memphis/bike-shop">
  This national outdoors store chain offers a local bike maintenance shop. They also have some good, affordable bike options.
</Card>
