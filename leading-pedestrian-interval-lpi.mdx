# Backstory

When I lived in Austin, I frequently crossed the large state-owned highway that I lived on (South Lamar). One crossing in particular was extremely sketchy, because the walk sign came on at the same time as the left turning light for the cars. A friend suggested that I log a 311 request to get a "leading pedestrian interval" (LPI) at this location, and I loved the result! I made a [TikTok video]([ttps://www.tiktok.com/@buttercreamgang/video/7215025178349178154]\(https://www.tiktok.com/@buttercreamgang/video/7215025178349178154\)) documenting it.
