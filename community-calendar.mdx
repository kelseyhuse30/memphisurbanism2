<Expandable title="Saturday, Sept 19 - The Legacy of 87 Adams">
  87 Adams is a memorial and public history project honoring the lives of the estimated 3,800 enslaved people who were bought and sold in the shadows of historic churches in Downtown Memphis. They are holding 3 historical tours in downtown Memphis with artists.<br /><br />RSVP [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-legacy-of-87-adams-program-and-historical-tour-tickets-2000203126395?aff=oddtdtcreator).
