title: "Memphis Urbanism"
---

Walking, biking, and taking public transit in Memphis **should be a lot better**.

Our public officials aren't prioritizing these issues, so it's up to us. This is a movement to:

1. Build political power to support walking, biking, and transit improvements in Memphis
2. Empower Memphians to improve their own neighborhoods, one small step at a time
