Filing all my paperwork to start a business was annoying as heck. So here I am documenting some lessons learned so that you don't have to make these same mistakes.

<Card title="Utilize the resources available to you" type="tip" horizontal>
  If you are unsure about something, please double-check with the government. The [TN Secretary of State](https://sos.tn.gov/contact) has a Business Services phone line. I have called it and gotten answers quickly - (615) 741-2286

  The City of Memphis has their own recommended steps for [Starting a Business](https://memphistn.gov/starting-a-business/)

  The [Shelby County Clerk](https://secure.tncountyclerk.com/index.php?countylist=79) has their own number - (901) 222-3000
</Card>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Business Address">
    If you're running a digital company (like me), you likely will not have a physical office address. But in Tennessee, you are required to file one. You can use your residential address, but you may not want that information to be public.

    I purchased a service from [Tennessee Registered Agent](https://tennesseeregisteredagent.com/) so that I can use their Knoxville address. If any legal mail is delivered there, I will be able to access it digitally. I can also use this as the physical office address on forms.

    For mailing address, I signed up for [My Memphis Mailbox](https://mymemphismailbox.com/) for \$10/month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organization Type">
    I registered as a [For Profit Benefit Corporation](https://sos.tn.gov/press-releases/for-profit-benefit-corporation-formations-now-available) which is a special TN business type. Filing is \$100 (versus \$300 for LLC). For Federal taxes, I believe these can be filed as an S-Corp. (I am still figuring this out)
  </Step>
  <Step title="EIN - Employer Identification Number">
    Do not pay for a service to receive an EIN, use the [Federal website](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) to get one for free quickly and easily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Local Business License">
    If making more than \$3K in sales per year, a city & county business [license](https://secure.tncountyclerk.com/newbusinesstax/index.php) is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accounting">
    You can use something simple like Google Sheets / Excel, or a free [Quickbooks ](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/)account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Banking">
    Once you have your EIN, charter, and state filing confirmation, you can open a bank account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credit Card">
    Once you have your bank account you can apply for a business credit card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website Domain & Business Email">
    I bought my domain from [namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/), and then purchased [Google Business Suite](https://referworkspace.app.goo.gl/woX2) to utilize gmail and the rest of the google suite. This requires manually setting up connections, which I feel comfortable with, but it depends on how familiar you are with DNS setup.
  </Step>

</Steps>
